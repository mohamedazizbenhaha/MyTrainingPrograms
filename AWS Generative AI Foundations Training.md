### AWS Generative AI Foundations Training Program

#### Program Overview
- **Target Audience**: Beginners in AI, developers, and professionals exploring generative AI on AWS.
- **Prerequisites**: None; suitable for newcomers to AI.
- **Delivery Format**: Instructor-led, online or in-person, with access to AWS Academy platform for student guides, demos, activities, and tools.
- **Objectives**: Understand AI/ML concepts, generative AI, prompt engineering, foundation models, responsible AI, security/governance, and application implementation.
- **Certification Alignment**: Prepares for AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01); includes knowledge checks and a dedicated prep module with practice exams.
- **Total Duration**: 20 hours.
- **Assessment**: Knowledge checks (quizzes) per module, demos, activities, and certification practice.
- **Materials Provided**: AWS Academy student guides, videos, demos, external activities, and playground tools.
- **Pricing Suggestion for Commercialization**: $400 (1200 TND) per participant (group discounts available); includes certification exam voucher option for +$100 (300TND).
